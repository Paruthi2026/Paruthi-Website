Google Sheet Inventory Setup Guide

Let any team member add outfits, update prices, or mark items Sold Out from a shared Google Sheet without touching HTML code.

3-Step Connection Setup:

  1. Open a new Google Sheet, click cell A1, and paste the headers copied below (columns A to L fill automatically).
  2. In Google Sheets, go to File → Share → Publish to web, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), click Publish, and copy the link.
  3. Paste that CSV link into googleSheetCsvUrl: "" inside STORE_CONFIG in this file.
Required Column Headers (A to L):
id,name,category,price,originalPrice,sizes,colors,stock,image,fabric,description,sizeGuide
  • • sizes: Separate age sizes with a pipe | (e.g. 6-12M|1-2Y|2-3Y|4-5Y).
  • • colors: Format as Shade Name:#HEX separated by | (e.g. Blush Peach:#F6B8A6|Berry Rose:#9E0B4A).
  • • stock: Type In Stock (or a number like 12). Type Sold Out or 0 to disable ordering automatically.
